Criminal Litigation

M.A.K. Ameen and Company provides representation and guidance for clients facing criminal matters, protecting your rights at every stage of the process.

Being charged with a criminal offence, or finding a loved one in that position, is one of the most stressful situations a person can face. Understanding your rights, what happens next, and having someone in your corner from the outset can make all the difference to the outcome of your matter.

M.A.K. Ameen and Company assists clients through the criminal justice process — from the moment of arrest or charge, through bail applications, trial preparation, and representation in court — with the same reliability, trust and integrity that defines the rest of our practice.

If you require assistance, please contact our offices as soon as possible. Time can be critical in criminal matters, so don’t delay in reaching out.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after someone is arrested?

By law, they must be brought before a court within 48 hours of arrest (excluding certain non-court days). They have the right to legal representation from the moment of arrest, and the right to apply for bail — either police bail for minor offences, or court bail at the first appearance or a later bail application.

Can you represent someone from the moment they're arrested?

Yes — the earlier we're involved, the more we can do to protect your rights, including attending the police station, advising before any statement is made, and preparing a proper bail application for the first court appearance.
