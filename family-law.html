Family Law

Are you getting married, divorced, both (we don’t judge)? Then you may need the services of M.A.K. Ameen and Company.

Family Law is that part of law that deals with matters such as divorces, antenuptial contracts, settlement agreements, maintenance orders, and more.

If you are about to get married you may want to consider getting an antenuptial contract drafted by the skillful hands of our attorneys. A good antenuptial contract may save you from a messy divorce, should worse come to worse.

If worse has already come to worse and you are about to embark on a messy divorce then M.A.K. Ameen and Company is here for you. Our skilled attorneys shall do our very best to ensure that your interests are protected during a divorce.
