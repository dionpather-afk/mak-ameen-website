Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an uncontested divorce take in South Africa?

Where both parties agree on the terms, an uncontested divorce through the Magistrate's Court typically takes around four to twelve weeks once all the paperwork is properly in order. Contested matters, or those heard in the High Court, take considerably longer.

Do I need an antenuptial contract (ANC) before I get married?

You're not required to have one, but without an ANC you'll automatically be married in community of property, meaning your assets and debts merge with your spouse's. An ANC lets you choose a different matrimonial property regime up front, which can matter a great deal if the marriage later ends.
