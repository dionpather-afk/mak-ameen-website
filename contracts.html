Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a contract have to be in writing to be legally binding in South Africa?

Not usually — most contracts are valid whether spoken or written, as long as there's clear agreement, both parties have the legal capacity to contract (generally 18 or older), and the purpose is lawful. Certain contracts, though, like the sale of property, long leases, and suretyships, must be in writing to be enforceable. We always recommend putting important agreements in writing regardless, simply because it's far easier to prove what was agreed.

Can you review a contract someone else has sent me before I sign it?

Yes — we regularly review contracts drafted by the other party to flag risky clauses, unclear terms, or anything that unfairly favours them, before you commit to anything.
