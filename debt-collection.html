Frequently Asked Questions

How long can someone legally chase me for an old debt?

Most ordinary debt “prescribes” (becomes legally unenforceable) three years after it became due — though certain debts, such as mortgage bonds and court judgments, run for thirty years. A letter of demand on its own doesn't restart that clock, but a payment or a clear acknowledgement of the debt can.

Someone owes me money — can you help me recover it without going to court?

Often, yes. We start with formal demand and negotiation, which resolves many matters without litigation. Where that doesn't work, we'll guide you through the correct court process for your claim, sized appropriately to keep costs proportionate to what's owed.
