About Us

M.A.K. Ameen and Company was started in 2003 by Senior Partner Mahommed Abdool Kader Ameen. He was later joined by his daughters Ms. Zaheera Ameen, and Ms. Faaria Ameen. Over the years M.A.K. Ameen and Company has built up a reputation of reliability, trust, and integrity.

While being a family run firm, they also extend this familiarity and hospitality to their clients. As a client of M.A.K. Ameen and Company you can expect the warmest service while we navigate the icy cold landscapes of the law on your behalf.
