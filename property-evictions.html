Property Management and Evictions

Ownership in property is known in law as a real right in property. It is the most complete real right that a person can have. It is a right enforceable against the world at large.

Therefore, ownership in property is one of the most valuable assets a person can have, second only to having a good legal team such as M.A.K. Ameen and Company. With M.A.K. Ameen and Company you can be at ease knowing your investments in property will be handled with the utmost skill and care in our experienced hands. Turn to us for transfers of property and property management.

In South Africa, we are all given the right to housing in terms of the Constitution. This right to housing must be balanced against the right to property. Contrary to popular belief, evictions are still viable in modern day South Africa, however certain requirements must be met. It is extremely important to begin eviction proceedings against an unlawful occupier before they have been in occupation of the land for more than 6 months! After 6 months, it is more difficult to evict an unlawful occupier! Therefore, contact us immediately if you have a tenant who is not paying rent!
