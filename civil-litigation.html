What We Do

Civil Litigation

M.A.K. Ameen and Company represents clients in civil disputes, helping to protect your rights and interests both in and out of the courtroom.

Civil litigation covers disputes between individuals, businesses or other parties where one side seeks a legal remedy from another — from contractual disagreements to disputes over property, damages or delivery of services. It can be a daunting process to navigate alone, which is why having experienced representation in your corner matters.

Our attorneys bring years of litigation experience to every matter we handle, guiding clients through each stage of the process — from the initial assessment of your case, through negotiation, and, where necessary, representation in court — with clarity, care and a focus on the outcome that matters most to you.

If you are involved in a civil dispute or believe you may have a claim, please contact our offices to discuss your matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which court will my case be heard in?

It depends on the value of your claim: under R12,000 generally goes to the Small Claims Court, up to R200,000 to the District Magistrate's Court, R200,000–R400,000 to the Regional Magistrate's Court, and anything above that — or more complex matters — to the High Court.

What's the first step if I need to sue someone, or someone is suing me?

Civil action begins with a summons, which sets out the claim and gives the other side a set period to respond. If you've received one, don't ignore it — strict deadlines apply, and failing to respond can result in default judgment against you.
