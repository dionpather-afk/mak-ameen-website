Road Accident Fund Claims

We deal with claims against the Road Accident Fund.

The Road Accident Fund is essentially a government run insurance company for anybody who was injured in a road accident. You may have a claim against the Road Accident Fund if you were seriously injured, or lost a breadwinner in an accident. You may also claim on behalf of your child if they were seriously injured in an accident.

In claiming from the Road Accident Fund, you will be able to claim damages for your injury, as well as medical expenses and loss of earnings if applicable. In the case of a deceased breadwinner, you will be able to claim loss of support and funeral expenses.

When claiming against the Road Accident Fund with M.A.K. Ameen and Company you will not have to pay in any fees*. We operate using a contingency fee agreement. This means that if your claim against the Road Accident Fund is not successful, you will not pay a cent. If it is successful our fees shall be 25% of the total claim.

Please note there are time limits in lodging claims against the Road Accident Fund! You must lodge your claim within 3 years from the date of your accident, or in the case of a hit-and-run accident, within 2 years.

If you believe you have a claim against the Road Accident Fund, please contact our offices.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to claim after a road accident?

In most cases you have three years from the date of the accident to lodge a claim with the Road Accident Fund. If the driver who caused the accident can't be identified (a hit-and-run), that window is shortened to two years — though minors and people under curatorship aren't bound by the shorter limit. Because these deadlines are strict, it's best to get in touch as soon as possible after an accident so nothing is missed.

What does “no fees unless we win” actually mean?

It means we act for you on a contingency fee basis — you pay nothing upfront, and our fee (capped at 25% of what's recovered, as required by law) only comes out of a successful claim. If we don't win, you don't pay us for the RAF claim itself.
